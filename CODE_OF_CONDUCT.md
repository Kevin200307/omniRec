# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which is incorporated here by reference. Where this document and
the Contributor Covenant differ, this document governs the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, and
  reviews.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback. Critique the work, not the
  person.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming a mistake was
  deliberate.
- Acknowledge the effort of others, including contributors who are new to the
  project.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as an address or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unsolicited contact.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository,
issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel used for the
project, and also when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions
that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation by anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening,
or harmful.

To report a violation, use GitHub's private reporting features for the
repository, or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Do not
report through a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
reporter's identity will not be disclosed without their consent.

Maintainers will consider each report, and will respond with the action they
consider appropriate. Anyone asked to stop a behaviour is expected to comply
immediately.
